## What the table tells you

1. **Top-tier candidates have zero confident-wrong topics.** That is the entire story of why they reach HMR 0.96+.
2. **Catastrophic candidates are killed by 1–2 Cnf-W topics, not by missed answers.** Error404-only has answered-accuracy 0.91 but loses HMR to BITEM (0.68 vs 0.96) entirely on R_O.
3. **Retrank-only's HMR (0.838) is partly metric-degeneracy:** 45% refusal rate buys it via the [HMR fragility caveat](#hmr-fragility).
4. **Some mid-tier candidates flip across judges.** Tier-1 broad scores 0.826 under Sonnet but 0.925 under Opus — see [Judge Cross-Validation](Judge-Cross-Validation.md).

## HMR fragility

A trivial always-refuse-at-conf-0.05 system also reaches HMR 0.974
with 0.0 accuracy and 100% refusal. Our system clearly distinguishes
itself on accuracy (0.94) and Cnf-W (0). The caveat: **HMR alone
cannot establish system quality** — you must read it jointly with
the companion measures in this table. See [Statistical Robustness](Statistical-Robustness.md).
