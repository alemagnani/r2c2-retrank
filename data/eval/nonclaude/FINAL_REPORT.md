# Non-Claude base-model swap: final report

## Experiment

This experiment tests whether the retrank answer-first, independent-verifier
confidence-calibration layer transfers from Claude to OpenAI models. The primary
criterion is whether calibration raises HMR for identical answers and correctness
labels, with the gain concentrated in overconfidence reward (`R_O`).

Three answer/verifier models were evaluated on all 65 NTCIR-19 R2C2 AC topics:

- `gpt-5.6-sol`: current flagship/SOTA condition.
- `gpt-5.6-luna`: efficient, much smaller condition.
- `gpt-4.1-mini`: cross-generation smaller-model control.

The fixed correctness judge for every condition was `gpt-5.5`. Because the judge
is held fixed and the ON/OFF runs use identical answers, nuggets, and correctness
labels, it cancels out of the primary paired comparison. Absolute HMR values should
only be compared with results scored by this same judge.

## Method

- Topics: 65, from `data/raw/r2c2topics.txt`.
- Passage pool: `BITEM-PG-1` plus `BITEM-PG-2`.
- Pool construction: passages sorted by supplied rank; top 20 retained per topic.
- CE reranking: disabled, as explicitly permitted by the experiment specification.
- Candidate answer and self-confidence: generated by the model under evaluation.
- Nuggets: 3–8 requested from the same model, grounded in the supplied passages.
- Independent answer: generated by the same model from nuggets only.
- Match score: 0, 1, or 2.
- Calibration ON: `min(c_self, {0:25, 1:60, 2:100}[match_score])`.
- Calibration OFF: raw `c_self`.
- Refusal rule: empty answer or no nuggets becomes `I don't know`, confidence 5
  when calibration is ON, and is always scored incorrect.
- Correctness: judged once per answer by fixed judge `gpt-5.5`.

The OpenAI Batch API was used for the passage-heavy candidate and nugget stages.
Batch stragglers affected the verifier stage, so the remaining verifier, judge, and
match requests were completed synchronously with bounded concurrency. This changed
transport and price, not prompts, models, data, confidence rules, or scoring.

One `gpt-4.1-mini` nugget response (topic `0020`) remained invalid after the initial
request and two retries. It was conservatively treated as no nuggets and therefore
as a refusal under the specified rule. No nugget or label was invented.

## Interpretation

The primary hypothesis is supported for every evaluated OpenAI model. Calibration
increased HMR for `gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-5.6-luna`, and `gpt-4.1-mini`, and `R_O`
increased in all three cases. This is the expected signature of the verifier cap
reducing confidence on incorrect answers.

The transfer is not limited to one model size. It appears in the GPT-5.6 flagship
and efficient variants, and the much larger effect on `gpt-4.1-mini` supplies a
cross-generation control. The strongest individual gain was `gpt-4.1-mini`, whose
HMR increased from 0.3989 to 0.8988 (`delta_HMR=+0.4999`) as `R_O` increased from
0.2500 to 0.8300 (`delta_R_O=+0.5800`).

For `gpt-5.6-sol`, calibration traded a small decrease in `R_U` (0.9990 to 0.9869)
for a larger increase in `R_O` (0.2850 to 0.4300), raising HMR from 0.4435 to
0.5990. `gpt-5.6-luna` showed the same shape: `R_U` decreased from 0.9948 to 0.9771
while `R_O` increased from 0.4067 to 0.5667, raising HMR from 0.5773 to 0.7173.

The primary claim should rest on the paired ON-versus-OFF deltas, not absolute HMR.
`gpt-5.5` is an OpenAI model and the fixed judge; this does not affect the paired
confidence comparison, but it matters for absolute cross-run comparisons. The
official field values of 0.761 (best non-retrank) and 0.963 (retrank Claude main)
were produced under a different official judging setup and therefore are context,
not strict like-for-like comparisons. Under this run's fixed judge, only
`gpt-4.1-mini` calibration ON exceeds 0.761; none reaches 0.963.

## Conclusion

Across all three tested non-Claude answer engines, the verifier-capped confidence
layer improved HMR and overconfidence reward. The result supports the claim that
the calibration mechanism transfers beyond Claude within this pipeline. The
consistent 3/3 positive HMR deltas and positive mean effects (`+0.2651` HMR,
`+0.2950` R_O) are the principal findings.
